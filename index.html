Minassa Dirasa

I kept losing track of things between an app, a file and a notebook, so I built one place for all of it. Minassa Dirasa has a timetable, a Pomodoro timer, tasks, notes and grades. It's in Arabic, and I keep adding to it.

ADHKAR BOT01 / 02

TELEGRAM BOT

Adhkar Bot.

Adhkar Bot keeps a Telegram channel alive without you. It posts the morning, evening and sleep adhkar, plus a Fajr reminder, at the times you choose. Set it up once and it posts every day on its own.

Open the bot @beta_adhkarbot
